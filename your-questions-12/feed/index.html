There are (of course) a couple of caveats;

  • escaping URLs has a different set of rules, but generally shouldn’t be such a big deal
  • occasional there are issues when stuff is converted by something that doesn’t understand UTF-8 (generally rare)
  • coding for pre-UTF8 devices like old-skool Japanese cHTML mobile phones which sometimes have one of the Japanese charsets instead

Check to make sure, but apart from that, hope you ♥ your more readable source! ☺❧
