]]> By: Jeremy Keith http://html5doctor.com/your-questions-answered-10/#comment-5823 Fri, 09 Jul 2010 13:55:11 +0000 http://html5doctor.com/?p=1991#comment-5823 Footer isn’t a sectioning root:

http://www.whatwg.org/specs/web-apps/current-work/multipage/sections.html#sectioning-root

You might have been thinking of sectioning content, but the only elements that are sectioning content are section, aside, nav and article:

http://www.whatwg.org/specs/web-apps/current-work/multipage/content-models.html#sectioning-content-0

Footer is flow content:

http://www.whatwg.org/specs/web-apps/current-work/multipage/content-models.html#flow-content-0
